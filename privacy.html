What this policy covers

This policy covers Flower Lab Shop's automated assistant in Instagram Direct and this website. If we add other messengers (Telegram, WhatsApp, Viber), we will update this page first.

What data we process

  • Your Instagram account identifier for our app. It is a number Instagram issues for our service only. We don't receive your password and don't fetch your profile.
  • The messages you send and our replies, with the time they were sent.
  • Photos and posts you send or forward. They are passed to the AI assistant to find similar bouquets, and we don't store them.
  • Service notes: the language you write in, and whether a florist has joined the conversation.

Why, and on what basis

  • To answer your questions, help you choose a bouquet and hand the conversation to a florist. These are steps you ask for before a purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep service quality up, prevent abuse and keep simple statistics, such as how many conversations needed a florist. This is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The AI assistant

  • First replies are written by an AI assistant, not a person. It tells you so if you ask.
  • The assistant only knows the bouquets we have in stock and information about the shop. A florist confirms price and availability before any order.
  • Write "human", "operator" or "manager" and a member of our staff will join the conversation.
  • The assistant makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Who else processes your data

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram) delivers messages between you and us. Meta processes them as a separate controller under its own policy.
  • Google Cloud hosts our service and database in EU data centres (Belgium and other EU countries).
  • Our AI service provider receives the conversation text and any photos you send, to generate a reply. Under its commercial terms, this data is not used to train its AI models and is kept only for a limited time for safety purposes.
  • Telegram: our private staff group. When a florist is needed, it receives the conversation identifier, a short summary of your request and the latest messages.
  • Our florists, who continue the conversation with you.

Our AI service provider processes data in the USA, and Telegram operates outside the European Economic Area. Transfers to the AI service provider are protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Telegram processes data under its own policy. You can ask us for the name of any provider listed here.

How long we keep data

  • Messages: 90 days after the last message in the conversation, then deleted.
  • A short summary of the conversation (2–3 sentences on what you were looking for and how it ended, without the messages themselves) and counts are kept for statistics for at most 3 years.
  • Photos you send are not stored by us.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no selling of your data.
  • No marketing profiles.
  • We don't message you first: the assistant only replies to your messages.

Please don't send payment card numbers, passwords or health information in the chat. They aren't needed to choose flowers.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and receive your data in a portable format. How to ask for deletion is described on the Data deletion page. For other requests, email reyfen.support@proton.me. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. In Ukraine: the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights, ombudsman.gov.ua.

Children

Instagram is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children younger than that.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We plan to add online ordering and payment. Before any new feature processes other data, we will update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Що охоплює ця політика

Які дані ми обробляємо

  • Ваші повідомлення і наші відповіді разом із часом надсилання.
  • Фото й дописи, які ви надсилаєте або пересилаєте. Вони передаються ШІ-асистенту, щоб знайти схожі букети, і ми їх не зберігаємо.
  • Службові позначки: мова, якою ви пишете, і чи долучився до розмови флорист.

Навіщо і на якій підставі

  • Щоб відповідати на ваші запитання, допомагати обрати букет і передавати розмову флористу. Це дії, про які ви просите перед покупкою (ст. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

ШІ-асистент

  • Перші відповіді пише ШІ-асистент, а не людина. Якщо ви запитаєте, він про це скаже.

  • Напишіть «людина», «оператор» або «менеджер», і до розмови долучиться працівник магазину.
  • Жодних рішень, які мають для вас юридичні чи подібно значущі наслідки, асистент автоматично не ухвалює.
